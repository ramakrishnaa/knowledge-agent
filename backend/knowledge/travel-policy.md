# Example Corp travel and expense policy (sample document)

This is a fictional policy included to demonstrate the knowledge base.

## Daily allowances

- Meals: 45 USD per day for domestic trips and 70 USD per day for international trips.
- Hotels: up to 180 USD per night domestically and 240 USD per night internationally.
  Stays in London, New York, Tokyo and Zurich may go up to 320 USD per night.
- Local transport (taxis, ride-hailing, public transit): up to 40 USD per day.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of
6 hours or more, and business class only for flights over 10 hours with manager approval.
Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance unless the trip is urgent.

## Submitting expenses

Submit receipts for every expense above 25 USD within 30 days of returning. Expenses submitted
after 60 days are not reimbursed. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## Approvals

Trips under 2,000 USD in total estimated cost need approval from the traveller's manager.
Trips of 2,000 USD or more also need approval from the department head.
